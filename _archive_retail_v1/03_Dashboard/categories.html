Revenue by category

Full year 2024, all 1,000 transaction lines. An even split would give each category 20.0% of revenue; the observed spread is narrower than a ranked bar chart makes it look.

This ranking is not a finding. A chi-square test on revenue by category returns p = 0.57: the order of these five bars is inside the range random variation produces. The widest gap between any two categories is about three points, a gap this data cannot distinguish from chance. Read the totals, not the order.

The hot and cold cells above are noise. A permutation test on this exact matrix returns p = 0.18: roughly one shuffle in five produces a category-by-city pattern at least as strong as this one. There is no evidence of a category-city affinity to act on, however striking a single dark cell looks.
