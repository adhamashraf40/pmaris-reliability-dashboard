City drill-down

Ten cities, one flat market

Every city's 95% confidence interval overlaps every other city's, so the apparent ranking from Dallas down to New York is not reproducible.

■ The widest city-to-city revenue ratio is 1.43× — and a gap that size is reproduced by chance in 35% of 20,000 simulations of the same data with the city labels shuffled. No city should be prioritised, funded, defunded or staffed differently on the evidence in this file.
