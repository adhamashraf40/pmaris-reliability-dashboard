Read the totals, not the order. Filtering changes which rows are counted, so every ranking on this page will reshuffle as you click. That reshuffling is the point: across nine tests the differences between categories, cities, genders, age bands, weekdays and months are all inside the range random variation produces. A bar that moves to the top under one filter has not become a better bet.

Age band and gender

Age bands use the ordered blue ramp; gender uses two fixed slot colours. Both are attributes of the transaction, not of a person.

Transaction volume against revenue, by city

If revenue were driven by anything other than how many transactions a city happens to record, points would scatter away from the diagonal. They do not: revenue tracks transaction count almost exactly. Axes are fitted to the data range, not zero-based. Click a point to filter.

Revenue by day of week

Calendar order, not ranked order. A weekday effect would show as a repeated shape; this is flat (chi-square p = 0.45 on the full file).
