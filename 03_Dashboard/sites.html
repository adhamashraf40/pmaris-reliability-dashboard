Sites & Equipment

Heavy industry carries more loss per window, but site explains little of it

The cement and drilling sites average about 1.5x the revenue loss of the lab and diagnostics sites, and the gap is statistically real. It is also small: site explains about 1.3% of the variation in loss, against 6.5% for spare-parts status. Criticality matters more for money: critical windows lose 4.4x what low-criticality windows lose.
