Levers · what management can move

Four levers separate the low-downtime windows from the high ones

Strategy, spare-parts availability, crew skill and PM discipline each split downtime by roughly 2x to 3x, and every one of those splits survives a Bonferroni-corrected test. Reactive runs 2.3x predictive, shortage 2.9x available, basic crews 1.8x expert.

⚠
ℹAssociation, not proof of cause. Reactive windows also start in worse health, so part of the gap is which assets end up reactive. The intervals below are study constant bootstrap results on the full file; the bars and curves are live.

So what. Moving reactive windows onto any planned strategy, clearing spare-part shortages and lifting PM compliance above 80% each target a measured 2x gap. Those are budget lines, not hopes, and they apply at every site.
